## Shortlist behavior

Maximum shortlist size: 3 positions.

When a fourth position is attempted, approved warning copy is:

3/3 positions selected. Please remove one from your shortlist to add another.

The shortlist summary displays saved positions and supports View, Remove, and Clear Shortlist.

Inquiry forms launched from a shortlisted position should show all shortlisted positions together by title and Position ID so the candidate understands that the conversation may address the full shortlist.

Approved shortlist inquiry message:

Interested in discussing the shortlisted positions and how my experience aligns with current opportunities.

## Filtering rules

Active geography and other active search filters must carry into Similar Positions. Similar Positions should not recommend positions outside the active filtered result set.

Discipline filtering must remain discipline-accurate. Mechanical / HVAC searches must not bleed into Plumbing or Fire Protection positions unless the underlying position is truly classified to match the selected discipline.

## Sharing and copy behavior

Position links and shortlist sharing should remain clean and human-readable for plain-text channels such as LinkedIn, SMS/text, and systems that strip rich formatting.

The goal is to avoid exposing long, intimidating raw URLs as the dominant visual element when a cleaner title / location / salary / Position ID presentation is possible.

## Prelaunch final test required

Before declaring production launch complete, perform one final desktop and mobile functionality / flow test covering:

1. Header navigation and anchor landing positions.
2. Search Careers behavior and whether it remains necessary on mobile while already inside Positions.
3. State, Discipline, Minimum Salary, Workplace, Market Sectors, and keyword filters.
4. View Positions button behavior after filtering.
5. Reset behavior, including whether reset should clear filters only or also shortlist state according to the approved workflow.
6. Position card View Position behavior.
7. Similar Positions respecting active search filters.
8. Shortlist add, remove, clear, and 3/3 warning behavior.
9. Inquiry modal shortlist summary showing all shortlisted titles and Position IDs.
10. Candidate inquiry submission, phone formatting, required-field validation, success message, turquoise styling, and confetti.
11. Client Hiring Support modal dropdowns, phone formatting, submission, success message, and confetti.
12. Reviews and AGILE Insights landing positions.
13. Contact landing position and Lilly contact links.
14. Footer buttons and footer copy.
15. Copy/share behavior for one position and three shortlisted positions.
16. Mobile text wrapping, sticky/header spacing, modal fit, and no section overlap.
17. Desktop section spacing, anchor offsets, and no excessive dead space.

## Launch rule

Do not declare the Careers site fully launched until the final functionality / flow test is passed on both desktop and mobile and any failures found in that test are corrected and rechecked.

After that pass, freeze a new dated LIVE baseline and proceed to the remaining pages on www.agileconsultingsolutions.com.
